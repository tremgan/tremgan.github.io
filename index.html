Things I've built

malt: Bayesian active learning for growth media

tremgan/malt

  • Proposes the next batch of media to run: a Bayesian Gamma GLM fit by MCMC in PyMC, with batches picked by q-Noisy Expected Improvement over the posterior draws.
  • Against response-surface DOE on simulated labs with a known optimum: median cumulative regret 43% lower over 81 runs where the Gamma GLM fits the truth, 20% lower where it doesn't.
